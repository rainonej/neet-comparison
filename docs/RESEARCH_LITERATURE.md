# Research literature map

## Medical education, merit, and finance

- **Financial barriers and inequity in medical education in India** (2024). Broad review of coaching, tuition, debt, and workforce diversity. Useful as a source map; fee figures must be traced to original documents.
- **Medicine, merit, money and caste: the complexity of medical education in India** (2010). Ethical framing of merit, private purchase, and reservation.
- **Towards a valid NEET-UG** (2025). Critique of construct validity, physics/chemistry emphasis, coaching, and dummy schooling.
- **Socioeconomic Inequality in Accessing Professional Higher Education in India** (2024/2026). NSS 75th analysis of gender, caste, and household economic status. Reproducible from public microdata.
- **Role of private sector in medical education and human-resource development for health in India** (2016). Historical growth and equity context.

## NEET-specific equity

- **Justice A.K. Rajan Committee report** (Tamil Nadu, 2021). Detailed admitted-student patterns by coaching, repeater status, school type, rurality, and parental income. Strong descriptive state evidence; weak applicant denominators and politically contested interpretation.

## Social mobility and labor markets

- **Intergenerational Mobility in India: New Measures and Estimates across Time and Social Groups** (AEJ Applied, 2024). District and group mobility; replication materials available.
- **Intergenerational education mobility in India: nonlinearity and the Great Gatsby Curve** (2024). IHDS analysis of persistence and subgroup gaps.
- **Patterns of social mobility and the role of education in India** (2016). Class/caste/locality/gender and occupational destinations.
- **An Overview of the Indian Health Labor Markets** (World Bank, 2025). PLFS-based wages, sector differences, and job quality for physicians and other health workers.
- **India Employment Report 2024**. Youth unemployment, informality, technical education, and consumption-quintile inequalities.

## Coaching and mental health

- **Assessment of anxiety among NEET-UG 2020 aspirants** (2023). n=200, single private college, cross-sectional.
- **Psychosocial Consequences of NEET Preparation** (2026). n=400 in one urban South Indian district; includes coaching/repeater and home-environment variables.
- **Psychiatric interventions and suicide prevention in India's coaching centers** (2025). Framework and policy critique rather than effect-size evidence.
- NCRB **Accidental Deaths and Suicides in India** reports. State/year administrative counts, not NEET-specific causal data.

## Methodological warning

Most NEET-specific studies are convenience samples, admitted-student analyses, reviews, or cross-sectional surveys. The repository should rank evidence as:

1. administrative population data;
2. nationally representative microdata;
3. state administrative data;
4. peer-reviewed observational studies;
5. convenience surveys;
6. advertising and journalism.
