# Data-request project descriptions

These are concise descriptions to paste into access-request forms. Adjust the applicant name and affiliation honestly.

## DHS / NFHS request

**Project title:** Socioeconomic opportunity, medical entrance examinations, and professional career pathways in India

**Purpose:** This noncommercial research project will construct a transparent Bayesian microsimulation of educational and occupational pathways surrounding India's NEET-UG medical entrance examination. NFHS household, individual, and geographic data will be used only to estimate anonymized distributions of household assets, wealth rank, education, social group, religion, family composition, rural/urban residence, and subnational context. These estimates will be combined statistically with separate public examination, counselling, education-cost, and labour-market datasets. No attempt will be made to identify respondents or link NFHS respondents to NEET candidates. GPS data will be used only for district/small-area contextual measures under DHS geographic-disclosure rules.

**Requested surveys/files:** India NFHS-4 and NFHS-5; household, household-member, women, men, and GPS files.

## PRICE ICE 360 request

**Project title:** Household affordability and educational mobility around medical entrance examinations in India

I am developing a noncommercial, open-methodology research model that translates the costs and uncertain benefits of NEET preparation and medical education into household-resource terms. I request research access to the 2014, 2016, 2021, and 2023 ICE 360 microdata, including household income, consumption, assets, debt, savings, education spending, labor-force participation, demographic profile, geography, panel identifiers, weights, questionnaires, and coding documentation. The data would be used only for statistical analysis and synthetic-population calibration. No respondent identification would be attempted, and licensed microdata would not be redistributed. Please provide the applicable academic/research terms, price if any, and whether derived aggregate results may be published with citation.

## Young Lives request

**Project title:** Family resources, aspirations, education, and early-career transitions in India

The project uses public longitudinal data to estimate transparent priors for the relationships among household resources, parental aspirations, cognitive achievement, school pathways, time use, educational expenditure, psychosocial outcomes, and entry into employment. These estimates will be used as partial evidence in a Bayesian simulation and will not be presented as nationally representative NEET-specific effects.

## CMIE quote request

Please provide an academic/noncommercial quotation and current variable dictionaries for Consumer Pyramids Household Survey products sufficient to analyze household income, household and individual employment, education and private-tuition expenditure, school/college attendance, professional education, debt/EMIs, household demographics, geography, and longitudinal household/person identifiers. Please state: years/waves included; unit-record versus aggregate access; number of users; export limits; permitted use of derived statistical models; restrictions on sharing with research collaborators; and whether short-term or project-specific access is available.

## AAMC aggregate-data request

**Purpose:** Construct a transparent U.S.-India comparison of how family background, educational cost, and admission probability shape access to medical careers.

Please advise whether AAMC can provide de-identified aggregate tables for recent entering classes crossing: applicant versus matriculant status; family-income quintile or bands; parental education; race/ethnicity; MCAT band; GPA band; state or broad region; public/private medical-school status; and debt or financing measures where available. Cell suppression is acceptable. The project does not seek personally identifiable or institution-identifiable applicant records. Please provide the available years, data definitions, privacy restrictions, and fee before producing a custom report.
