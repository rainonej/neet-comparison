# Multilingual search log

Searches were conducted in English, Tamil, Malayalam, and Hindi. This file records both positive and negative findings so later work does not repeatedly search the same paths and mistake search-engine silence for absence.

## Tamil

Representative concepts searched:

- `NEET தரவு மாணவர் வருமானம் பயிற்சி சேர்க்கை ஆய்வு தமிழ்நாடு`
- `நீட் அரசு பள்ளி மாணவர்கள் சமூக பின்னணி மதிப்பெண் பயிற்சி`
- `நீட் தேர்வு ராஜன் குழு தரவு 2583 மாணவர்கள்`
- `மருத்துவ சேர்க்கை பெற்றோர் வருமானம் தமிழ் வழி ஆங்கில வழி`

Findings:

- Tamil-language reporting led to the 2,583-student government/aided-school cohort used by the A.K. Rajan Committee.
- It surfaced government-coaching programme counts and discussions of Tamil- versus English-medium admission representation.
- Tamil Nadu government archives are the richest identified source for socioeconomic admissions analysis.
- No publicly indexed candidate-level file crossing income, coaching, medium, score, and admission was found.

## Malayalam

Representative concepts searched:

- `NEET അപേക്ഷകർ വരുമാനം പരിശീലനം പ്രവേശനം ഡാറ്റ കേരളം ഗവേഷണം`
- `NEET സർക്കാർ സ്കൂൾ വിദ്യാർത്ഥികൾ ഡാറ്റ കേരള മെഡിക്കൽ പ്രവേശനം`
- `കീം മെഡിക്കൽ റാങ്ക് വിഭാഗം അലോട്ട്മെന്റ് ഡാറ്റ`
- `കേരള നീറ്റ് സൗജന്യ പരിശീലനം എസ് സി എസ് ടി ഒ ബി സി`

Findings:

- Kerala CEE/KEAM provides unusually structured public rank, category, allotment, last-rank, and vacancy pages.
- Kerala government pages identify free NEET coaching opportunities for SC, ST, and OBC students, which creates potential programme-cohort leads.
- Kerala Employment Department records can provide counts of registered job seekers by education/profession and social group, with the important caveat that registrations are not unemployment rates.
- No public Kerala file linking household resources or coaching to NEET score and medical admission was found.

## Hindi

Representative concepts searched:

- `नीट अभ्यर्थी आय कोचिंग प्रवेश डेटा शोध`
- `नीट सामाजिक आर्थिक पृष्ठभूमि अंक मेडिकल प्रवेश अध्ययन`
- `नीट आरटीआई परिवार आय कोचिंग खर्च`

Findings:

- Search results were dominated by individual success stories, current-affairs reporting, coaching pages, and exam controversies.
- The central Ministry of Education RTI archive was more useful than Hindi-language forums.
- No hidden national socioeconomic candidate dataset or credible public mirror was identified through Hindi search.

## Forum and social-media search

Forum searches produced valuable qualitative themes—repeat years, parental sacrifice, coaching relocation, language barriers, and counselling confusion—but no verified national microdata source. Forum material should be used to design narrative scenarios or interview questions, not to estimate probabilities.

## Overall conclusion

Multilingual search improved discovery, especially in Tamil and Malayalam, but did not reveal a lawful national applicant-level socioeconomic file. Tamil Nadu is the strongest lead for administrative microdata; Kerala is the strongest lead for clean public admissions records.
