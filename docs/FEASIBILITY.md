# Feasibility assessment

## Feasible now

### National NEET score distribution
The 2024 anonymized centre release contains approximately 2.33 million marks. It supports empirical score distributions nationally and by test-centre geography. It does **not** include domicile, caste, gender, income, school board, coaching, attempt number, or admission outcome.

### Seat and college capacity
NMC college records and annual matrices can produce state, management, college, and intake tables. Institutes of National Importance must be reconciled separately through MCC records.

### AIQ and central/deemed admission cutoffs
MCC final allotment and admitted-candidate PDFs contain ranks, categories, quotas, colleges, courses, and rounds. These can reconstruct much of the national 15% AIQ and central/deemed allocation system.

### State admission cutoffs
Most states publish some combination of merit lists, allotment PDFs, seat matrices, and prospectuses. Collection is feasible but requires a state-by-state adapter because portals, PDF layouts, identifiers, and archival practices differ.

### Household-resource distributions
HCES, AIDIS, NFHS, and IHDS can generate state and rural/urban household distributions for consumption, assets, debt, caste, religion, family size, and parental education. These households are not identifiable as NEET applicants.

### Coaching costs
CMSE 2025 and NSS education rounds measure private coaching participation and expenditure nationally. Commercial program price lists can augment the upper tail and NEET-specific menu of prices. Neither source supplies a nationally representative causal coaching effect on NEET rank.

### Career outcomes
PLFS supports wage and employment distributions for physicians and plausible alternate occupations by state, sex, urbanity, sector, education, and social group, subject to sample-size limits. Hierarchical partial pooling will be necessary.

## Feasible with calibrated assumptions

- Probability of choosing science in Class 11-12 conditional on family background.
- Probability of aspiring to medicine.
- NEET score shifts associated with coaching and repeat attempts.
- Repeat-versus-alternative decisions after a failed attempt.
- Family borrowing and asset-sale responses to coaching/private tuition.
- College completion and transition into employment.

Each calibrated link needs an uncertainty distribution and sensitivity mode. A single point estimate would be misleading.

## Not identifiable from current public data

- Causal effect of barely obtaining an MBBS seat on later earnings or household mobility.
- National admission probability conditional jointly on income, caste, gender, school board, location, coaching, and attempt number.
- National coaching effectiveness after controlling for baseline ability and family selection.
- Individual psychological or suicide risk from a failed or cancelled attempt.
- Exact city-level household income percentiles for all Indian cities.

## Recommended first empirical scope

1. Use 2024 as the exam/admissions cohort because the anonymized centre-score release is unusually detailed.
2. Build national score and seat tables.
3. Complete AIQ/deemed/central allotments nationally.
4. Select three state systems with different publication quality and institutional structures:
   - Tamil Nadu: unusually rich policy analysis and public selection lists;
   - Kerala: generally structured entrance/counselling publication;
   - Gujarat or Maharashtra: substantial government/private mix and different fee structures.
5. Estimate household and career distributions nationally with state random effects.
6. Treat coaching effects as sensitivity parameters until an applicant-level dataset or credible quasi-experiment is found.
