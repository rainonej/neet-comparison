# RTI request templates for missing NEET administrative data

## Important eligibility note

The Government of India's central RTI Online portal is for **Indian citizens**. It covers central public authorities and must not be used for state-government authorities. A state request must go through that state's RTI mechanism. The filer should use their own identity and should never misstate citizenship.

RTI generally provides records that already exist; it does not require an authority to conduct a new statistical analysis. For that reason, each request asks first for schemas, existing electronic exports, and existing tabulations. It offers privacy-preserving alternatives such as banding and small-cell suppression.

## Template 1 — National Testing Agency

**Public authority:** National Testing Agency

**Subject:** NEET-UG application database fields and anonymized application-result records, 2019-2026

Under the Right to Information Act, please provide the following records in electronic form for each NEET-UG year from 2019 through 2026:

1. The complete data dictionary, database schema, field list, code list/value labels, and record-retention schedule for the NEET-UG online application, examination-result, and candidate research/analytics databases.
2. Copies of any existing anonymized candidate-level research datasets, internal statistical extracts, cross-tabulations, reports, presentations, or analyses that relate application-form background fields to score, percentile, rank, qualification, or state/category results.
3. If an existing electronic database export can be provided after severing identifying fields under Section 10, please provide an anonymized CSV/XLSX export containing only the fields already held by NTA from the following list: examination year; marks or marks band; percentile/rank band; qualified status; gender; category; PwD status; place of residence; permanent state and district; present state and district; annual family-income band; father's/guardian's occupation and qualification; mother's/guardian's occupation and qualification; mode of preparation; question-paper medium; Class 10 and Class 12 year, school type, board, state and district; and whether/where the candidate appeared in a previous NEET-UG examination.
4. If item 3 cannot be disclosed at row level, please provide any existing aggregate tables crossing score/rank/qualification bands with the listed background fields. Small cells may be suppressed, and exact scores/ranks may be grouped to protect privacy.
5. Please identify the division and officer that maintains these databases and any policy governing their use for educational research.

I do not seek names, application numbers, addresses, telephone numbers, email addresses, Aadhaar/PAN/passport information, photographs, biometrics, school roll numbers, or any other direct identifier. Please provide records in their existing electronic format. If any portion is exempt, please sever the exempt portion and provide the remainder, stating the specific exemption applied.

## Template 2 — MCC / DGHS

**Subject:** Machine-readable NEET-UG counselling, allotment, joining and vacancy records, 2019-2026

Please provide, in the existing electronic format, for each UG counselling year 2019-2026:

1. Data dictionary/schema and code lists for candidate registration, choice filling, allotment, admission/joining, resignation, upgradation and vacancy databases.
2. Existing de-identified electronic records or exports containing: year; counselling round; course; candidate rank or rank band; category; quota; PwD status; domicile/state if held; gender if held; allotted college/institute; college state; seat/management type; allotment status; joining/reporting status; resignation/withdrawal/upgradation status; and final admitted status.
3. Round-wise and institution-wise counts of allotted, joined, not joined, resigned, upgraded and vacant seats by course, quota/category and management type.
4. Existing fee/seat matrices and college master tables in CSV/XLSX or database-export form.

Direct personal identifiers are not requested. Small-cell suppression or rank banding is acceptable.

## Template 3 — National Medical Commission

**Subject:** Historical college-year medical education capacity, cost, completion and attrition records

Please provide existing electronic records and data dictionaries for each medical college and academic year from 2010-11 through 2026-27 containing, where held:

- college identifier, name, state, district, ownership and management type;
- sanctioned, permitted and recognized MBBS intake;
- actual admitted students;
- annual examination candidates and pass counts;
- internship entrants/completers;
- graduates/pass-outs;
- discontinuation, attrition or seat-lapse counts;
- tuition/mandatory fee information and internship stipend information;
- recognition, increase/decrease, closure or penalty decisions.

If NMC does not hold a requested field, please state that fact and identify the public authority or statutory reporting system believed to hold it. Please provide machine-readable files rather than scanned printouts where those files exist.

## Template 4 — state counselling authority

**Subject:** State NEET-UG applicant, merit, allotment, joining, fee and vacancy records, 2019-2026

Please provide the existing electronic data dictionary and de-identified records for all applicants and counselling rounds for MBBS/BDS admissions in the state for 2019-2026. Requested fields, where collected, are: year; state merit rank; NEET rank/score band; domicile/nativity; state reservation category and subcategory; EWS/income band; gender; PwD; government-school quota; school type, board and medium; Class 12 passing year; repeater/previous-attempt status; course; college; ownership/management/fee category; allotted round; joined/not joined; resigned/upgraded; and final admission status.

Please also provide the college-seat-fee master, round-wise vacancy records, and applicant/allotment/joining counts by the listed categories. No names, contact information, addresses, application numbers or other direct identifiers are requested. Row-level data may use randomized record IDs; small cells may be suppressed.
