# Researcher outreach request template

Use a narrow request. Researchers are much more likely to share a codebook, aggregate table, or execute a small analysis than to transfer an unrestricted raw file.

## Core request

I am building a transparent, noncommercial research model of the economic and educational choices surrounding NEET-UG. The project separates observed data from assumptions and will not publish individual identities or predict individual mental-health outcomes.

Your study appears to contain variables that are otherwise unavailable in public NEET statistics. Would you be willing to share one of the following, in descending order of usefulness?

1. the anonymized analytic dataset and codebook under whatever research-use terms you require;
2. a restricted data-use agreement;
3. the questionnaire, variable definitions, and analysis code;
4. a small set of deidentified cross-tabulations or model coefficients;
5. execution of a short analysis script against data that cannot leave your institution.

The most useful fields are broad demographic and family-background categories, school type/board/medium, coaching type and duration, repeat attempts, NEET/JEE distinction, exam score or score band, admission outcome, and the psychological or academic measures reported in your paper. I do not need names, roll numbers, dates of birth, addresses, or contact information.

I will cite the study and dataset exactly as you specify and will not redistribute restricted files.
