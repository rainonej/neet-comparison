A fair paper is not a fair race.

NEET is India’s once-a-year national entrance exam for undergraduate medicine. A clean paper matters. It is not enough.

  • Same paper. Unequal runways.Money, language, geography, coaching, and years available shape who can compete.
  • “Qualified” is not a seat.Millions clear a minimum score. Far fewer affordable government MBBS places exist.
  • Coaching becomes the entry fee.Families pay to stand still while seats stay fixed.
  • The rank launders privilege.The number belongs to the student. The conditions that produced it did not.
