India’s national medical entrance exam · NEET-UG 2026

The paper leaked. The exam was cancelled. Students died. The incompetence is obvious. That is why people are in the streets.

But even a clean exam is not a fair race.

2.33 million candidates · 180 questions · 3 hours · about 130,000 medical places

NEET gives every student the same paper—but not the same years of coaching, language access, family money, or ability to try again.

  • About 18 candidates compete for every MBBS place.
  • An affordable government-college place is scarcer still.
  • One guessed answer can create a five-mark gap.
