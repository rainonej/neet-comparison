Medicine really does buy mobility, respect, security, and income. The system that rations it is cruel: a heavily taxing lottery that sells better odds to families who can afford the ticket.

See the prize — then the trap

01 · Why people chase it

The prize is real.

This is not a story that medicine is a scam. An MBBS seat is a genuine ladder: status, job security, quality of life, and higher earnings than most alternate paths. Families are not irrational for wanting it. That is exactly why the rationing hurts.

02 · The lottery

Too few seats for a fair race.

Call it an exam if you want. Mechanically it is a mass lottery with a rank ticket. In 2024, more than two million appeared for roughly one hundred thirty thousand MBBS seats. Most diligent students will lose — not because they are worthless, but because the funnel is that thin.

Qualify is not the prize

Roughly 56% “qualify.” There are still about 10 qualified students for every MBBS seat. The soft qualify line flatters the system. Families care about a seat they can actually take.

02b · Razor margin

One question can be a life.

NEET marks are mostly high-school Biology, Chemistry, and Physics — +4 for a correct answer. Under national capacity accounting, the any-MBBS seat line sits near ~560 marks. The median appeared candidate is about ~99 correct-answers-equivalent below that line. Near the line itself, the gap between a seat and another year (or giving up) is absurdly small.

Candidates within ±N correct answers of the any-MBBS line

Interpolated from national marks quantiles. “Questions” = marks ÷ 4 (one extra correct, holding others fixed). Not the same as counselling cutoffs by state/category.

Why this feels like a lottery

When tens of thousands of students sit within a few questions of the seat line, tiny noise — one silly mistake, one lucky guess, one coaching drill — rearranges futures. That is not “clear merit separation.” It is a knife-edge rank auction.

Sample items?

Official NEET items are copyrighted, so we do not paste them here. The pedagogical point still holds: the exam is largely Class 11–12 syllabus content. The cruelty is not that the questions are PhD-hard — it is that high-school trivia margins decide who gets the mobility prize after years of extraction.

03 · Buying better odds

Privilege does not invent the prize. It loads the dice.

A private offer a family cannot pay is a ticket they cannot cash. English-medium schooling, metro prep markets, and the ability to buy coaching or private seats do not replace “merit” — they purchase better chances inside a scarce lottery, then the winners look like they earned it alone.

Claimed

One exam. Same paper. Rank decides. Meritocracy.

Actual machine

Scarce seats + years of prep cost + private fees. A lottery you can afford to bias.

The laundering

Winners are praised as meritorious. The unequal ticket price disappears from the story.

04 · The tax

Everyone pays. Rank barely moves.

Coaching is the entry fee of the lottery. Holding others fixed, more prep can raise your absolute score. When the whole pool escalates, relative ranks barely move — while money and years are still extracted from families who mostly will not get a seat.

Accessible-seat odds when the pool arms up

Unilateral advantage vs everyone-escalates worlds. The tax keeps rising even when the positional gain collapses.

What the winners look like

In Tamil Nadu, nearly all admitted students had coaching in one reported year — and ~71% of admits were repeaters. The winning pool is soaked in prep and second chances. That is composition, not proof that coaching “caused” each seat — and it is exactly how privilege gets laundered into a merit story.

What families actually spend

National coaching participation and spend among enrolled students inform intensity priors. Median positive prep spend anchors the two-part score shift.

05 · Years as currency

We do not know how many times people sit.

NTA does not publish attempt counts or age. What we have is admitted composition (TN: ~71% repeaters among winners) and labeled continuation scenarios. Success exit = joined an acceptable seat — not “qualified.”

Observed

TN admitted repeater share 71% [Rajan21]. Elite-admit samples can look closer to ~1/3 repeaters. Heterogeneity is huge.

Missing

National P(sittings=1,2,3…). Best ask: prior appearances × Class XII year × score band from NTA.

Wrong model

Constant geometric retake probability. Continuation should fall with attempt, cost runway, and burnout.

06 · Evidence grain

What the numbers can defend.

The emotional claim is sharp; the measurement is not magic. Scarcity and the prize are solid. Privilege-weighted odds are a calibrated synthetic ladder. Coaching effects are skeptical priors. Causal “return to a seat” is not identified.
