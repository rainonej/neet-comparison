Qualify is
not a seat.

Medicine really does buy mobility, respect, security, and income. Seats are scarce, preparation is costly, and private capacity is unequally affordable — so an offered seat a family cannot cash is not access.

See the prize — then the trap

01 · Why people chase it

The prize is real.

This is not a story that medicine is a scam. An MBBS seat is a genuine ladder: status, job security, quality of life, and higher earnings than most alternate paths. Families are not irrational for wanting it. That is exactly why the rationing hurts.

02 · The funnel

Too few seats for a fair race.

Call it an exam if you want. Mechanically it is a mass rank tournament over scarce seats. In 2024, more than two million appeared for roughly one hundred thirty thousand MBBS seats. Most diligent students will lose — not because they are worthless, but because the funnel is that thin.

Qualify is not the prize

Roughly 56% “qualify.” There are still about 10 qualified students for every MBBS seat. The soft qualify line flatters the system. Families care about a seat they can actually take.

02b · Razor margin

National capacity leaves a thin mark margin.

NEET marks are mostly high-school Biology, Chemistry, and Physics — +4 for a correct answer. Mapping all nationally approved MBBS seats onto the national marks distribution yields a capacity-equivalent threshold near ~560 marks. The median appeared candidate is about ~99 correct-answers-equivalent below that line. That is an accounting quantile — not a state/category counselling cutoff — but it shows how thin the national funnel is even before domicile, quotas, fees, and rounds.

Candidates within ±N correct answers of the any-MBBS capacity threshold

Interpolated from national marks quantiles. “Questions” = marks ÷ 4 (one extra correct, holding others fixed). Not a counselling cutoff by state/category.

Why the margin is thin

When tens of thousands of students sit within a few questions of the capacity threshold, small score differences correspond to large rank differences. This page does not yet model day-of-exam shocks or test–retest reliability — only the thinness of the national capacity margin.

Sample items?

Official NEET items are copyrighted, so we do not paste them here. The pedagogical point still holds: the exam is largely Class 11–12 syllabus content. The cruelty is not that the questions are PhD-hard — it is that high-school trivia margins decide who gets the mobility prize after years of extraction.

03 · Buying better odds

Privilege does not invent the prize. It shifts competitive position.

A private offer a family cannot pay is a ticket they cannot cash. English-medium schooling, metro prep markets, and the ability to buy coaching or private seats can purchase better chances inside a scarce rank tournament — without turning rank into a pure measure of individual merit. 04 · The tax

Everyone pays. Rank barely moves.

Coaching can be the entry fee of a rank tournament. Holding others fixed, more prep can raise your absolute score. When the whole pool escalates equally (everyone intensive), relative ranks barely move — while money and years are still extracted. Rivals escalate keeps your labeled prep while others arm up.

Accessible-seat odds when the pool arms up

Unilateral vs universal intensive vs rivals-escalate. Universal prep collapses relative coaching advantage; rivals-escalate penalizes the under-prepped.

What the winners look like

In Tamil Nadu, nearly all admitted students had coaching in one reported year — and ~71% of admits were repeaters. The winning pool is soaked in prep and second chances. That is composition, not proof that coaching “caused” each seat — and it is exactly how privilege gets laundered into a merit story.

What families actually spend

National coaching participation and spend among enrolled students inform intensity priors. Median positive prep spend anchors the two-part score shift.

05 · Repeater tax (observed)

Most TN winners were not first-sit school-leavers.

Among Tamil Nadu MBBS admits in 2020–21, only 28.6% were current-year students and 71.4% were classified as repeaters [Rajan21]. That is admitted composition — a birth-year / Class XII proxy — not a national applicant attempt histogram.

07 · Evidence grain

What the numbers can defend.

The emotional claim is sharp; the measurement is not magic. Scarcity and the prize are solid. Privilege-weighted odds are an ordered scenario pathway on a fixed-reference threshold model with labeled evidence classes. Coaching effects are plug-in skeptical priors. Causal “return to a seat” is not identified.
