The Accessible Seat

Privilege and coaching shift who can take a medical seat they can actually afford. Mobility is real. Meritocracy-as-description-of-access is not.

Enter the funnel

01 · National ecology

One exam. Extreme scarcity.

In 2024, more than two million students sat NEET-UG. About one hundred thirty thousand MBBS seats exist in the national capacity account. Qualifying is common. Getting a seat is not.

Qualify ≠ seat

Roughly 56% qualify. There are still about 10 qualified students for every MBBS seat. The story that matters for families is not the soft qualify line — it is whether they get a seat they can take.

Source: reconciled NTA appeared/qualified counts and NMC seat capacity. Capacity cutoffs are national accounting shares (seats ÷ appeared), not state or category counselling pools.

02 · The right outcome

Accessible, not merely offered.

A private offer that a family cannot pay is not access. Government seats are scarce. Private capacity widens offers — then affordability decides who can walk through the door.

What we count

P(accessible MBBS seat) = government offer, or private offer that clears the affordability filter. Private offers that fail the filter do not count.

What we refuse

We do not treat “share of admitted students who were coached” as P(seat | coaching). That is the winning pool, not the applicant pool.

05 · Coaching arms race

Prep helps you — until everyone prep’s.

Holding others fixed, prep raises absolute scores. When the whole pool escalates equally, relative ranks barely move while costs still rise.

Accessible-seat probability by stratum × arms-race world

Toggle worlds above in the ladder panel, or compare all three here. Skeptical coaching prior (conservative profile) — not a NEET LATE.

Composition fact

In Tamil Nadu, nearly all admitted students had coaching in one reported year — and ~71% of admits were repeaters. That describes the winning pool. It does not identify the causal effect of coaching or mean attempts among applicants.

06 · After the seat

Wages by profession — not by college ownership.

Once on a path, earnings are modeled by career. Government and private medical colleges share the same physician wage prior. Private mainly buys access for families who can pay.

Median annual earnings if employed (₹ lakh)

Monte Carlo medians from privilege model. Bars show p25–p75. Zeros are a separate story.

Annual zeros are an employment / non-completion filter for young adults — usually family support or delayed independence, not street poverty. Do not lock zeros into a 35-year lifetime NPV.

07 · Years of trying

What one number can — and cannot — tell you.

We observe the share of admitted students who were repeaters. Turning that into the share of applicants who are repeaters needs an assumption about relative admit odds (ρ).

Sensitivity

Formula: a = r / (r + ρ(1−r)), with r = 71.4% among admitted (TN 2020–21). This does not identify mean attempts or the full 1,2,3… histogram.
